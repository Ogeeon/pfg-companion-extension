# Privacy Policy: PfG Companion

Last updated: 2026-11-03

PfG Companion is a browser extension that collects data from your OGame account and makes it available to ProxyForGame. This policy describes what it does with that data.

## What is collected

While you are logged in to OGame and browse its game pages, the extension reads account data that OGame provides to your browser: your player ID, your universe, your planets and moons (names, coordinates, resources, fields, temperatures) and the order of your planet list, production settings, active production boosters and species bonuses. It does not read passwords, cookies or messages, and it does not keep a record of your browsing history.

## Where it is stored

The data is stored only in the extension's local storage in your browser. The extension sends nothing to its developer, and it does not use analytics or advertising services.

## Requests to OGame

The extension makes requests only to OGame's own game server, the same kind the game itself makes. Your account information is requested once per game session, and production settings are read only from the Resource Settings page that you open. The extension does not change your selected planet and does not poll the game in the background.

## Use by ProxyForGame

When you open proxyforgame.com in the same browser, the ProxyForGame site reads the stored data from your browser so that it can use your account. The extension does not transmit the data to any server itself. How ProxyForGame handles the data after that is described in its own privacy policy: https://proxyforgame.com/policy.php.

## Your control

- The panel in the OGame menu has a Clear button, which removes the stored data for the current account.
- Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.

## Sale and sharing

Data is not sold, not used for advertising or tracking, and not shared with third parties other than the hand-off to ProxyForGame described above.
